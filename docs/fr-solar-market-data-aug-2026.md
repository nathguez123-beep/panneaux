# French residential solar — verified market data, August 2026

Research for `panneaux` content. Every figure carries a date and a source. **Re-verify before publishing anything older than ~3 months** — this market just moved hard.

---

## ⚠️ The June 2026 reform — read this first

The **arrêté du 1 juin 2026** (JO 4 June, in force **5 June 2026**) restructured the S21 regime. Independently confirmed across trade press and installer sources:

| What | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Prime à l'autoconsommation | 80 €/kWc (3–9 kWc) | **Abolished** for new applications |
| Surplus buyback tariff | ~4–4,73 c€/kWh | **1,1 c€/kWh**, 20-yr contract, +2%/yr indexation |
| Vente totale ≤9 kWc | Permitted | **Banned** — surplus-only |

**Grandfathering:** installations whose *demande complète de raccordement* was filed **before 5 June 2026** keep the old terms for the full 20 years.

### Why this is the opportunity, not just the bad news

A large share of French solar pages still publish pre-June figures. Verified stale example: Ohm Énergie still states "80 euros du kilowatt-crête" and "0,04 €". Every one of those pages is now **wrong**, and an AI assistant answering "quelle prime pour les panneaux solaires en 2026" is choosing between them.

A site that publishes correct, dated, post-reform numbers is the obvious thing to cite. That is the entire GEO play, handed over.

**This window closes.** The advantage decays as competitors update. Weeks, not months.

**Content hooks this creates:**

- "Prime à l'autoconsommation supprimée — ce qui change" (high search volume, most results stale)
- "J'ai déposé mon dossier avant le 5 juin — que se passe-t-il ?" (grandfathering; anxious, high-intent audience)
- "Le solaire est-il encore rentable en 2026 ?" (the question everyone now has)

## Battery storage

- Lithium-ion **700–1 000 €/kWh**; LiFePO4 700–1 300 €/kWh
- 2–6 kWh installed: **2 000–6 000 € TTC** · 6–10 kWh installed: **6 000–12 000 € TTC**
- **Tax trap worth its own page:** adding a battery to the same operation pushes the **whole project to 20% TVA**

## Payback

Post-reform, honest range: **10–16 years.** 9–12 in the sunny South/Southwest, 12–16 average zones.

Treat the 8-year figures still circulating as pre-reform. ADEME's 14-year figure is Dec 2024/Jan 2025 — label it as pre-reform if used.

## Self-consumption without a battery

**30–40%** (ADEME, ~35% baseline). Multi-orientation south-east-west adds ~6 points. Moving 25%→45% cuts payback by ~5 years.

Do not publish the 61% figure one source gives — it is an outlier assuming optimized behaviour.

## Admin

- **Déclaration préalable de travaux**, not permis de construire, for panels on an existing roof
- Instruction: **1 month**, **2 months** in a secteur protégé / ABF zone
- Silence = tacit non-opposition; worth requesting an *attestation de non-opposition* from the mairie

## RGE / QualiPV

QualiPV is the PV-specific RGE qualification (Qualit'EnR / Qualibat, standard NF X50-091). Since **1 March 2026** the installer must hold it for the client to get **5,5% TVA**. Its old role as gatekeeper to the prime is moot — the prime is gone.

## Do NOT publish without further confirmation

- The **10% TVA** claim for 12 kWc — one source says yes, the majority say the binary 5,5%/20% split. Conflicting.
- Whether any **quarterly tariff revision** mechanism survives the reform — appears replaced by fixed rate + 2%/yr, but worth checking Legifrance directly. *(Update 12 Aug: confirmed replaced — fixed 1,1 c€/kWh with 2%/yr indexation, no quarterly revision.)*
- The déclaration préalable detail — service-public.fr fiche F1996 returned 404, so it is unverified against the primary state source.
- 12 kWc pricing — single source only.
